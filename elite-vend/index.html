EliteVend

Four moves. About ten seconds.

  1. Tap

    Tap your card or phone on the reader. That unlocks the door.

  2. Open

    Pull the door open like a fridge at home. Everything is in reach.

  3. Grab

    Pick it up, read the label, change your mind. Take one thing or six.

  4. Go

    Close the door and walk away. You are charged only for what you kept.

The tech inside

You shop.
The machine does the math.

  • It sees what you take

    Cameras and shelf sensors recognise each item the moment it leaves. Put something back and it comes straight off your tab.

  • One tap covers everything

    Card or phone wallet. No coins, no codes to punch in, no second transaction for the second item.

  • Stocked by data, not guesswork

    Every sale updates the inventory count. We know what is running low before the shelf is empty, and what your building actually buys.

  • Never closed

    Night shift, early gym session, late deadline. The market is open whenever the building is.
