The tech inside

It sees
everything

Cameras and shelf sensors track every item from the moment it leaves the shelf. No barcodes, no checkout, no honour system.

  • Item level vision

    Each product is recognised as it moves. Put one back and it drops off your total.

  • Live inventory

    Every sale updates stock in real time, so shelves get refilled before they run empty.

  • One tap, any basket

    Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Take one item or ten on a single tap.
